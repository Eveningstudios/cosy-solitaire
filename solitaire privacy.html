Privacy Policy

Cosy Solitaire (on the App Store as “Solitaire – Cozy Card Game”) · Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: Cosy Solitaire doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Cosy Solitaire is made by Cozy Cards, a small independent game maker. If you have any question about this policy, see Contact below.

Information we collect

None. The game doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't create an account for you.

Your progress stays on your device

Your games, settings, tokens, trophies, card decks, level and statistics are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They're never sent to us. If you delete the app, this saved progress is deleted with it.

No adverts, analytics or tracking

The game shows no adverts and contains no analytics or advertising tools. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone. There are no in-app purchases.

Internet connection and game updates

The game works fully offline. When it's opened with an internet connection, it may briefly check for small game updates using a service called Expo, run by 650 Industries, Inc. To answer that check, Expo receives basic technical information such as the device type, operating system and app version. This isn't used to identify you. You can read Expo's privacy policy at expo.dev/privacy.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports to help fix problems. These come from Apple, not from the game, and you can turn them off at any time in your device's settings.

Children

Cosy Solitaire is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
