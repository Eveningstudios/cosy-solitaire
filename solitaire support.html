Cosy Solitaire (on the App Store as “Solitaire – Cozy Card Game”)

Questions

How do I play?

Cosy Solitaire is classic Klondike solitaire. Build each of the four Ace piles up by suit, from Ace to King. In the seven columns, place cards in descending order, alternating red and black. Only a King can go into an empty column. Tap the deck to turn over cards. Tap a card to send it to the best place, or drag it. “How to play”, on the Settings tab of the menu, explains it all.

What's the difference between Draw 1 and Draw 3?

Draw 1 turns over one card at a time from the deck; Draw 3 turns over three, and only the top one can be played, which makes it harder. Open the menu (the sliders button at the top of the screen) and choose on the New game tab. Your choice applies from the next game.

Why can't I win every game?

Deals are random, just like shuffling a real deck, so some games can't be won however well you play. Hint and Undo are always there, and you can start a new game whenever you like.

How do I get tokens, and what are they for?

You earn a token every time you win. Spend tokens in the shop (the coin button) on new places to play, cosy touches for each place, and win animations. Tokens can only be earned by playing. Nothing in the game costs real money.

How do I unlock card decks and trophies?

Card decks unlock by completing challenges, such as winning at a particular place, winning in under three minutes, or winning without using Undo. Trophies are won at each place: bronze for your first win there, then silver and gold as you win more.

What are levels?

You earn experience as you play, and more when you win. Each new level updates your Milestone deck, which grows from wood to bronze, silver and gold.

If I change place partway through a game, does the win count?

Before your first move, changing place simply moves the game there. After you've started, the game will ask: you can start a fresh game at the new place, or carry on. If you carry on, a win still earns its token and experience, but doesn't count towards that place's trophy or deck.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device, including any game in progress. It isn't stored online, so if you delete the app, your progress is deleted too.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline.

Are there adverts or in-app purchases?

No. There are no adverts and nothing to buy, ever.
